Why this matters: Docker publishes ports through its own iptables chains, so a container port bound to 0.0.0.0 is reachable from the internet even if it is not in TCP_IN. Ports bound to 127.0.0.1, or reached only through the Coolify / Traefik proxy, are not. Restrict adds DOCKER-USER rules (saved in csfpost.sh) that drop external traffic to that port while keeping container-to-container and localhost access.
